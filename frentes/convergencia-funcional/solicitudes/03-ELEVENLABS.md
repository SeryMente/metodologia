## Borradores completos · solo afirmaciones sostenibles

### Product description

Khora is an early-stage personal cognitive-support software project focused on retrieving dispersed ideas, developing writing grounded in the user's own sources, and reviewing suggested next steps. We are investigating whether voice capture and spoken playback can make this process easier without losing uncertainty, negation, authorship, or the ability to return to the original source. Voice features remain under evaluation rather than validated product claims.

### Why ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is relevant because speech recognition, speech generation, and conversational tools could support a coherent voice experience instead of a one-off narration. We would use any approved support only for a defined product-development task, with synthetic or explicitly authorized data, and would assess fidelity, review effort, latency, consumption, and the cost of continuing after the grant.

### Current progress and evidence

We have documented the initial functional research and prepared a synthetic text-evaluation protocol. We have not completed comparative voice testing or established clinical effectiveness, verified customer traction, or superiority over existing services. The next evidence step is a bounded demonstration of a genuinely useful task, not a claim that voice or a knowledge graph is inherently novel.
